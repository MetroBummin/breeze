## What is being withheld

72 of the 73 pending articles fail the extraction gate's confidence >=.75 check.
Five also fail promotion confidence and one mismatch confidence. This identifies the gate that withheld them, not its underlying cause or evidence
that those articles are low quality. It does not establish that the threshold is
too strict, that extraction is defective, or that model confidence is calibrated. The rule explicitly avoids excluding long/difficult/sensitive
subjects, but available inventory still loses useful long reporting: all 12
ProPublica pieces remain pending, including long medical/investigative content.
No automatic confidence reduction is justified by this unlabeled sample.

(RSS-011/043/045/047/102; raw responses intentionally not retained, so exact

RSS-094 (the old audit independently observed a truncated STEM list). This run's

NASA diagnosis: public DNS returned `www.nasa.gov → 192.0.66.108` and
`science.nasa.gov → 192.0.66.161`. The same `publicAddress` function returned false
for both because it blocked **all 192.0/16**, rather than special-use 192.0.0/24

cannot reach extraction. A proposed fix and regression on the separate evaluation
branch keep private/mixed DNS answers blocked. It was **not deployed** and the
six articles were **not re-evaluated**; original results stay immutable.

Jev budget. Durable fixed slots prevent duplicate paid attempts across readers,
client restarts and errors. A repeated POST for RSS-105 returned the saved verdict

not an invoice. Source: https://docs.typesafe.ai/models.md (checked 2026-10-02).
