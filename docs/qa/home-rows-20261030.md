# Home: 추천 (고전 / 웹소설 / 기사) · 내 책 (2026-10-30)

PR #307, revised after owner feedback round 2. Screenshots are in
[`home-rows-20261030/`](home-rows-20261030/) and are made with
`node tools/capture-home-rows.mjs` (Chromium; the 기사 tab shows live public
RSS, so the cards differ from run to run). Each run seeds one started Holmes
book (42%), one started pasted article (18%) and one finished text, so 내 책
shows its order: unfinished items first, most recently opened first.

Home shots are full-page, so the floating dock appears where the viewport
ended when the shot was taken.

Web-novel covers are typographic covers made by
`node tools/make-webnovel-covers.mjs`: title and author only, 768 × 1152 WebP
like the classic covers. The colour and ornament come from each story's
synopsis (Royal Road listing, the author's site, the store page). Mr. Penn's
cover (ivory invitation card, oxblood ink, sealed envelope) follows its
opening: 150 wedding invitations in May 1899, then letters to a newspaper.
Royal Road's robots.txt asks AI assistants not to read chapter pages, so
chapter text was not read; chapter titles come from the public feed. The authors' cover art is not used or imitated.

Checks: `node tests/verify-home-rows-browser.mjs` and
`node tests/verify-casual-rails-browser.mjs` (Chromium and WebKit),
`node --test tests/verify-home-recommend-kind.mjs tests/verify-web-novels.mjs`.
